# R4.0 Limitations & Threats to Validity

1. Host-assisted framework ≠ fully autonomous in-kernel OS.
2. Single-threaded interpreter; no SMP/hardware atomicity.
3. Two policies only; fixed HXF workloads; no parametric sweeps.
4. Smoke n=3 descriptive statistics; no significance claims.
5. Policy-ID rollback ≠ full VM restore.
6. ready_seq wrap fail-closed; finite counter.
7. External validity beyond catalog workloads unproven.
8. Related-work citations in paper draft are placeholders until verified.
